## The Channels Your Purchase Unlocks

Buying the crash course gives you a role, and that role makes a second set of channels visible at the bottom of the channel list.

There are three of them.

**`#crash-course-general`** is the default. Course chat and announcements go here.

**`#crash-course-random`** is for hanging out with the rest of the crash course community, off topic.

**`#crash-course-questions`** is for questions about specific lessons.

If you cannot see those channels, the role has not attached to your account. Click the link below the video. It hooks you up with the correct role, and the channels appear.

## One Question, One Thread

`#crash-course-questions` is a forum channel, not an ordinary one. You do not post a message into it. You create a post.

You give the post a title, which is the thing you need answering, then a description, which is the detail. The post opens as its own thread, and everything that follows stays attached to it: the replies, the back and forth, the fix.

This keeps the channel organised. One question, one thread, one place to look. When somebody else hits the same wall, they find the question and the answer together instead of an answer floating loose in a busy channel.

![#crash-course-questions forum channel showing a post open as its own thread](https://res.cloudinary.com/total-typescript/image/upload/v1786109257/ai-hero-images/ebfuvim9xc016wb2fyp7.png)

## Roles In The Server

Two roles are worth knowing about.

**Heroes** carry a red name. Alper is a hero. A hero is someone I have designated because they have been consistently helpful, or shown something genuinely good. It is given, not applied for.

**Moderators** carry a purple name. If you notice something that needs moderating, spam being the usual case, you can mention the `@moderator` role and somebody will deal with it.
