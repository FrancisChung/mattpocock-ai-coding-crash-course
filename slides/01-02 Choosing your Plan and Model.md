# Choosing Your Plan and Model

Before you begin this AI coding course, you need to set up your AI access. This means choosing the right subscription plan and understanding what you'll need.

## You Need a Paid Plan

Unfortunately, you need a paid plan to complete this course.

The free tiers of the major model labs are not sized for [agentic](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/agent) work of this kind. You will hit a wall in the first serious [session](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/session).

## Anthropic Pricing

Looking at [Anthropic's pricing page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11049762-choosing-a-claude-plan), the free tier won't be enough to complete the course.

**Pro** is just enough, and that's where I recommend you start. It gives you access to a mid-tier [model](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/model) that will get you through the course.

If you're doing this work professionally, I recommend upgrading to **Max**. Max comes in two flavors: 5X or 20X.

![Anthropic's pricing page showing Free, Pro, and Max subscription tiers](https://res.cloudinary.com/total-typescript/image/upload/v1785914282/ai-hero-images/tvc8kwnln1wqholvfdtc.png)

## OpenAI Pricing

OpenAI's pricing is similar, with an additional Go tier in the middle.

![OpenAI's pricing page showing Plus, Go, and Pro subscription tiers](https://res.cloudinary.com/total-typescript/image/upload/v1785914283/ai-hero-images/ayofpodmv1vza2xaush2.png)

## What I Used While Recording

This course was recorded with a 20X Anthropic Max subscription.

However, the maximum you'll need is a 5X. That's about £89 per month (roughly $100).

## Subscriptions vs API Keys

You might be wondering how subscriptions compare to API keys, where you pay per [token](https://www.aihero.dev/ai-coding-dictionary/token).

My advice: get a subscription. It's a much better deal - often 20 to 50X cheaper, depending on your tier and how much work you fit into the usage limits.

![Tweet from Theo showing $50,000 in token spend equating to $800 in subscriptions](https://res.cloudinary.com/total-typescript/image/upload/v1785914284/ai-hero-images/scdpmcotjexz8yr5p08b.png)

For example, Theo came close to $50,000 in token spend in a single month. With subscriptions, that would have been just three $200 Claude subs plus one $200 Codex sub.

That's $800 for around $50,000 worth of token spend.

## The Psychological Impact of Pay-Per-Token

There's also a psychological impact when you see the cost of every session.

It's like having an electricity meter at home - you become reluctant to use any electricity, and it changes how you work. You optimize for tokens instead of efficiency.

You'll make slightly irrational decisions when focusing on cost instead of quality. You might drop to a cheaper model just to keep the number down.

With a subscription, you know upfront what you'll pay and what you'll get. No budget stress.

Use the default model that comes with your plan. If you're on Pro, avoid Opus - it will drain your usage limit very quickly.

We'll revisit model choices later. For now, lock in a subscription before you start.
